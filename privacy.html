Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains what data the DigiHub Fusion application ("the Application") collects when you connect it to QuickBooks Online, Shopify, or a courier account, why we collect it, how it is protected, and how you can have it removed.

We do not sell your data, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not use your QuickBooks data to train machine-learning models. Data from one customer is never visible to another.

1.Who we are

DigiHub Fusion is the data controller for the information described here. You can reach us at privacy@digihubfusion.com.

3.Legal basis

We process this data to perform our contract with you, and, for security logging and fraud prevention, on the basis of our legitimate interests. Where your end customers' personal data (buyer name, address, phone) passes through the Application, we act as a processor on your behalf and only on your instructions.

4.How data is stored and protected

  • All traffic between your browser, the Application, and third-party APIs uses TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Data is held in a managed PostgreSQL database with encryption at rest and restricted network access.
  • OAuth tokens and API keys are stored encrypted and are never displayed in the interface or in logs.
  • Application secrets are held in environment variables, never in source control.
  • Access to production systems is limited to named administrators and protected by multi-factor authentication.
  • Each customer's records are isolated by company identifier and enforced on every query.
  • Backups are encrypted and retained for 30 days.

If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authorities without undue delay and within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

5.Who else sees the data

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the Application: our cloud hosting provider, our managed database provider, and our transactional email provider. Each is bound by contract to protect the data and may use it only to deliver their service to us. We also disclose data where required by law. Our servers are located outside Pakistan; transfers are protected by standard contractual clauses with those providers.

6.How long we keep it

Operational data is retained while your account is active. When you disconnect the Application from QuickBooks Online, the stored tokens for that company are deleted immediately and API access stops. When you close your account, all remaining data for that account is deleted within 30 days, and purged from encrypted backups within a further 30 days. Anonymised, aggregated usage counts that cannot identify you or your customers may be retained.

7.Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect at any time — in QuickBooks Online go to Apps → My Apps → DigiHub Fusion → Disconnect, or use the Disconnect button inside the Application.
  • Access or export — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correct or delete — ask us to fix inaccurate data or erase your account data.
  • Object or restrict — ask us to stop a particular processing activity.

Email privacy@digihubfusion.com and we will respond within 30 days. Deletion requests are confirmed in writing once completed.

8.Cookies

We use only strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in and to protect against cross-site request forgery. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

9.Children

The Application is a business tool and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10.Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will notify you by email or in the dashboard at least 14 days before the change takes effect, and update the date at the top of this page.
