Connect your QuickBooks company

Courier accounts are kept per company.

Aaj ke Shopify orders laayein — or drop a Shopify export — and the router splits the day between your courier companies by city, by the share you set each one, and by what went wrong there last time. Every account gets its own sheet in that courier’s own column order. What was handed over afterwards is on Pickups; where each courier will go is on Service areas.
