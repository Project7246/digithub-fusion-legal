checking Report
When an order is created in Shopify, create an invoice in QuickBooks Online, numbered … on terms …
Shopify tells us the moment it happens, so nothing is polled and nothing waits for a person. A number keeps its # if you give it one — QuickBooks counts 9323517 and #9323517 as two different invoices, so this must match what was uploaded before.
Left blank, the terms are whatever QuickBooks has on the customer.
Associate the invoices with the customer …, and put the buyer’s own name and address in the billing block
One customer for the shop, not four thousand of them. The buyer is not lost: their name and address are written on the invoice itself, and nothing goes in the memo.
{mon}{yy} becomes Oct26, so each month has its own customer. Plain text is one customer for ever. Typed once and kept.
Match all associated products on the SKU, and nothing else — and never create a product in QuickBooks
Where the SKU on the Shopify line is the SKU on a QuickBooks item, that item is used, and what it says is what goes on the invoice: its name, its sales description, its account. The Shopify title is not consulted at all, so a product renamed on one side cannot become a second item on the other. Its purchase description is left exactly as it is — that belongs on bills, not here.

The price is the one exception, and it comes from Shopify: what the buyer was actually charged is a fact of that sale, not a setting in the books. Cost is never touched. Cost reaches QuickBooks when the purchase order is entered, and nothing on an invoice has any business writing it.

A SKU that is on no item in QuickBooks, and a Shopify line with no SKU at all, stop that order and name it in the report. Nothing is guessed and nothing is part-written.
This was a setting once, and it is the one thing on this page that could put the wrong product on real books. Matching on the name made a second item carrying the same SKU, and the sales went to the new one. So it is not a choice any more. If something needs fixing from that, Put it back lists exactly what Fusion made.
Put the order’s shipping on the item …, and the discount in the discount box, in rupees
The delivery line reads its description out of QuickBooks too, by the same rule as every other line. Left blank, Fusion looks for Shipping, Delivery, Shipping Charges or Freight and uses whichever it finds — it does not make one.
Leave out cancelled orders
A cancelled order is never written at all — an invoice for an order that was called off is simply wrong.
Void means refunded in full or voided in Shopify. Unfulfilled means not shipped yet — worth leaving out where the money only lands on delivery.
