With this on, an order placed on Shopify becomes an invoice in QuickBooks by itself, within seconds. The page where you pick dates stays for filling in a month that was missed.

Which orders

Nothing placed before this day is ever written, so turning the writing on does not go back over every order already here. What to leave out — voided, refunded, not shipped yet — is ticked on the orders page, beside the days, and the same ticks apply here. Cancelled orders are never written at all.

What the invoice says

Who the invoices are made out to, what goes before the number and where delivery lands are set on the orders page, beside the button that makes them — one place, and the automatic writing uses the same answers.

A product that is not in the books yet

Products are matched on the SKU and nothing else — the same SKU is used on both sides. A product QuickBooks has never heard of is made when an invoice needs it, as the same kind of thing it is in Shopify: stock-tracked there, inventory here. There is nothing to choose, because choosing could only disagree with the shop.
