01The shops

One row per shop, so a client with two storefronts keeps them apart. Type the shop’s address and press Connect: Shopify will ask you to allow it, and hands the key straight to Fusion. Nothing is copied or pasted, and what is kept here is encrypted.

03What each product is in QuickBooks

Matched on the SKU, and nothing else — the same SKU is used on both sides, so there is nothing to set up and nothing to get wrong. A product QuickBooks has never heard of is made when the invoice needs it, as the same kind of thing it is in Shopify: stock-tracked there, inventory here. This list is only to show you what will happen; the dropdown is there for the odd product you want to overrule.

05Into QuickBooks

The invoices are built here and handed to the upload page, which is where they are actually written — so Stop, Resume, the log and the retry of whatever failed are the same ones the rest of the app uses. An order whose number is already an invoice in QuickBooks is left alone.
